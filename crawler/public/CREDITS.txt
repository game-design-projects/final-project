DONJON: CREDITS

Monster AI: Jev, the System One model by TypeSafe (https://typesafe.ai)

Music (ambient.m4a, battle-*.m4a, fanfare.m4a, victory.m4a)
- Original music made for this game, on the game's own melody. Rendered offline by
  scripts/build-ambient.py, build-battle.py and build-stings.py (the sampler is scripts/sampler.py).
- Instruments: harpsichord, church organ, string ensemble, choir aahs, music box and tubular bells
  samples from the MusyngKite soundfont, via gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts. CC BY-SA 3.0:
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/
- Kettle drums and tabor: synthesised in scripts/sampler.py, no samples.

Sound effects: synthesised live in the browser (sfx.js), no samples.

Rules inspired by the Dungeons & Dragons 5th Edition System Reference Document.

Fonts: Press Start 2P and VT323 (SIL Open Font License), via Google Fonts.
