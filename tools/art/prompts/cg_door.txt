Primary request: the Last Door. At the end of a high stone hall stands an enormous arched door of black stone with two keyholes side by side, framed by carvings of little figures standing in pairs, hand in hand. It is shut tight. Two small hands rest flat on the door from the bottom edge, alone. The two torches either side burn low and their flames, which have tiny eyes, look down sadly. Nobody else anywhere in the picture.
