What the game ships, who made it, and under what licence. Every asset in assets/ is named here, and the licence texts that must travel with the game are in assets/LICENSES/ and assets/fonts/.

MONSTER AI
- Jev, the System One model by TypeSafe (https://typesafe.ai). The monsters think with it over the network; nothing of the model ships with the game.

ART (assets/art/*.png)
- The drawings are the project's own: drawn for this game with OpenAI's image generation (Codex's built-in image tool) from the prompts in tools/art/prompts/, kept as source sheets in tools/art/source/ and cut into the game's atlases by tools/art/process.py (see docs/ART.md).
- The clan lords and the choice screens' pictures (assets/art/lord_slime.png, lord_bandit.png, lord_rock.png, lord_snake.png, events.png): drawn for this game by Abdulla Alfalasi with ChatGPT's image generation, kept as tools/art/source/lords.png and events.png and cut by the same tools/art/process.py.

OPENING FILM (assets/video/intro.ogv)
- Generated with ByteDance's Seedance video model through Dreamina, from reference crops of the game's own drawings and a text prompt (docs/intro/seedance-prompt.md), then re-encoded to Ogg Theora.

MUSIC: every place and every foe (assets/audio/music/*.ogg)
- Original music made for this game: a tune for each biome, each clan's hall, the trader, the practice floor and the test room, for every kind of monster and every champion, and the victory and stairs stings. Written and rendered offline by tools/music/build-themes.py (the player is tools/music/orchestra.py, the mixing and the hall tools/music/sampler.py) and encoded to Ogg Vorbis by tools/music/to_ogg.sh.
- Its instruments are recordings from the Versilian Studios Chamber Orchestra 2, Community Edition (VSCO-2 CE, https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE), released under Creative Commons CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication): strings, brass, woodwinds, harp, glockenspiel, marimba, kettle drums, snare, tambourine, triangle and cymbals by Versilian Studios, and the organ sampled by Simon Dalzell of Ivy Audio. CC0 asks for nothing; both are credited because they ask it as a courtesy.
- The audio files are the project's own recordings of that music, shipped under the game's own terms.

MUSIC: the hum theme, at the camp (assets/audio/hum-explore.ogg)
- Written for this game by Abdulla Alfalasi: an exploring loop on his hummed melody. His MIDI, source and render script were contributed to this repository (tools/music/hum-theme/, with a README beside them), and the loop is the project's own recording: it ships under the game's own terms, and no separate licence is granted for reusing it elsewhere.
- Rendered with GeneralUser GS v2.0.3 by S. Christian Collins (https://www.schristiancollins.com, https://github.com/mrbumpy409/GeneralUser-GS). Its licence: "You may use GeneralUser GS without restriction for your own music creation, private or commercial." It asks for no credit; this is good form.

SOUND EFFECTS
- Synthesised when the game starts (src/audio/sfx.gd, src/audio/synth.gd), no samples.

MONSTER VOICES (assets/voices/): every line rendered ahead by an open text-to-speech model, each monster's voice cloned from a reference recording in LibriTTS-R. The reference clips are in tools/voices/refs/ (trimmed, mono, 24 kHz); the rendered lines are new recordings in a voice like the reference speaker's, then pitch- and tempo-shaped per monster and loudness-normalised (tools/voices/render.py, tools/voices/voices.json).
- assets/voices/cosyvoice3/: CosyVoice3 (FunAudioLLM/Fun-CosyVoice3-0.5B-2512, https://github.com/FunAudioLLM/CosyVoice), Apache License 2.0. The engine of the Feiyu voice stack; the Feiyu voice library itself is not used.
- assets/voices/qwen3/: Qwen3-TTS (Qwen/Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-0.6B-Base, https://github.com/QwenLM/Qwen3-TTS), Apache License 2.0.
- assets/voices/gemini/: Google Gemini TTS (gemini-3.8-flash-tts, prebuilt voices Leda, Algenib, Enceladus, Orus and Charon), reached through OpenRouter; not cloned from a reference. Generated audio used under the Gemini API terms.
- Reference voices: LibriTTS-R (https://www.openslr.org/141/) by Y. Koizumi, H. Zen, S. Karita, Y. Ding, K. Yatabe, N. Morioka, M. Bacchiani, Y. Zhang, W. Han and A. Bapna, Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/); derived from LibriTTS and LibriSpeech, from LibriVox recordings. Changes: the clips are trimmed, made mono and resampled to 24 kHz, and used as voice references rather than played. Its full text is assets/LICENSES/CC-BY-4.0.txt. - ONNX Runtime, copyright (c) Microsoft Corporation (https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime), MIT License. Its licence and third-party notices ship beside the helper as LICENSE-onnxruntime.txt and ThirdPartyNotices-onnxruntime.txt.
- Speech model: NVIDIA Nemotron Speech Streaming, nvidia/nemotron-speech-streaming-en-0.6b (https://huggingface.co/nvidia/nemotron-speech-streaming-en-0.6b), in sherpa-onnx's int8 export (https://huggingface.co/csukuangfj2/sherpa-onnx-nemotron-speech-streaming-en-0.6b-160ms-int8-2026-04-25).
