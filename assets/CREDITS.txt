Monster AI: Jev, the System One model by TypeSafe (https://typesafe.ai)

Music (assets/audio/ambient.ogg, battle-*.ogg, fanfare.ogg, victory.ogg)
- Original music made for this game, on the game's own melody. Rendered offline by
  tools/music/build-ambient.py, build-battle.py and build-stings.py (the sampler is tools/music/sampler.py)
  and encoded to Ogg Vorbis by tools/music/to_ogg.sh.
- Instruments: harpsichord, church organ, string ensemble, choir aahs, music box and tubular bells
  samples from the MusyngKite soundfont, via gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts. CC BY-SA 3.0:
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/
- Kettle drums and tabor: synthesised in tools/music/sampler.py, no samples.

Sound effects: synthesised when the game starts (src/audio/sfx.gd, src/audio/synth.gd), no samples.

Hold to talk (desktop builds): the speech is recognised on your own machine, nothing is sent anywhere.
